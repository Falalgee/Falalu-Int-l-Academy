Academic Excellence

A seamless educational journey from early childhood through to senior secondary school, designed to make every child future-ready.

Ages 6 months – 5 years

Creche & Nursery School

Our Early Childhood Development programme provides a warm, nurturing, and stimulating environment where our youngest learners can explore, play, and grow. We focus on building foundational skills through structured play and guided discovery.

Programmes Offered

Early Childhood Development Phonics & Literacy Basic Numeracy Social Development Creative Arts Music & Movement Play-based Learning Communication Skills
Primary 1 – 6

Primary School

The Primary School years are critical for building strong academic foundations. Our curriculum is designed to foster curiosity, critical thinking, and a lifelong love for learning while ensuring every child masters core competencies.

Core Subjects

English Language Mathematics Basic Science & Tech Social Studies Civic Education Computer Studies P.H.E. CRS / Islamic Studies Cultural & Creative Arts Nigerian Languages
JSS 1 – 3

Junior Secondary School

During these formative years, we broaden our students' horizons. The JSS curriculum introduces a wider range of subjects to help students discover their strengths and interests before specializing in Senior Secondary School.

Core Subjects

English Language Mathematics Basic Science Basic Technology Business Studies Computer Studies Social Studies Civic Education Agricultural Science Home Economics French Religious Studies
